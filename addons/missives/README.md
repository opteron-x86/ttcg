# Tessera — Missives

Collectors can post requests for Tessera cards on Missives boards. Bring the requested card to the collector for extra gold or a card from their collection.

Requires **Tessera 0.32.4 or later** and **Missives 2.03 SSE**. Install this as a separate mod and enable `Tessera Missives.esp` after both. The plugin is ESL flagged.

## Playing

1. Own a Tessera Album and check a Missives board. Notices appear through the board's normal refresh, so an existing board may need its next refresh first.
2. Read the notice to see the card, collector and reward. Take it to accept the quest.
3. Speak to the collector and choose **About the Tessera card you wanted.** Confirm the exchange to finish the quest.

Only a standard copy counts. Foils are kept. If the card is used in a saved deck or giving it away would leave fewer than five playable cards, the confirmation warns you.

The collector keeps the card you deliver and can use it in games. A card offered as a reward is set aside until the notice is completed or cancelled. Gold offers pay roughly three times a normal card sale, with a minimum of 50 and a maximum of 2,500 gold.

There can be one collector notice per hold. Notices use Missives' **Easy Quest Chance** and **Quest Refresh** settings. By default, that means about a 50% chance when you approach a board after its three-day refresh interval. A refresh can also remove an unaccepted notice. Accepted notices have no deadline and remain until completed or cancelled. Reopen a notice in your inventory to discard it; cancelling returns any reserved reward to the collector. A collector's death also cancels the request.

Requests cover all tiers and rarities, including non-unique Legendary cards. A card obtained only through a quest can also be requested; you must still obtain it through its usual route. Requests use standard copies, never foils. Uniques and cards marked unavailable or unreleased are excluded. Each notice keeps its agreed card and reward. The initial addon covers the nine Skyrim holds; it does not add boards to Solstheim or new lands.

## Testing a notice now

For an immediate Whiterun test, use the console command `startquest TesseraMissivesJob1`, then check Whiterun's board. You still need the album. For another hold, use Job2–Job9 in this order: Falkreath, Haafingar, Hjaalmarch, The Pale, The Rift, Winterhold, Eastmarch, The Reach.

Try a gold delivery, a card trade, cancelling a notice, and saving/reloading with an accepted notice. The board will otherwise use its normal Missives chance and refresh settings.
