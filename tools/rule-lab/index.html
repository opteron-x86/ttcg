    The rules

    Place one card each turn. A higher facing value captures an adjacent opponent’s card. After nine placements, the player controlling more cards wins; the last card in hand also counts.

    Ascension / Descension

    Count each affinity across both players’ placed cards. Matching cards in both hands and on the board gain or lose 1 per card, up to 3. Neutral cards are unaffected. A card with multiple affinities uses its largest matching count once.

    Dominion

    A card loses 1 from all sides when at least two adjacent cards belong to the same group and it does not belong to that group. One Nord and one Khajiit do not weaken an Orc; two Nords do. Only touching sides count.

    Nord 0—Orc −1—Nord 0

    Every group can do this from the start. Both players’ cards count, including your own. The penalty is always −1, even beside more cards or two qualifying groups. Capturing a card does not change its group.

    Timing & other rules

    The new card counts before captures resolve. Changing values never flips unrelated cards by itself. Values can go below 1 or above A. Reverse makes lower values win. Same and Plus use printed values; their Combo captures use effective values.

    At the table

    Hover over an empty square after selecting a card to preview the move. Outlined targets would be captured. Effective values include every modifier; Printed shows the original card values. Undo takes back your turn and the computer’s reply. Play both hands gives you control of both sides.
