Custom card backs

Put PNG, JPG, JPEG or WebP images in this folder, then open Tessera Settings
and select Refresh beside Card back. Choose an image to save it as your back.
The filename becomes its label. Underscores display as spaces.

Use portrait artwork, ideally 3:4 (for example 768 x 1024).
Images are fitted to the card. No ESP edit or recompilation is needed.
To replace an image in place, select Refresh again.

In a mod manager, you can keep your images in a separate mod at:
TesseraUI/art/backs/custom/

If the selected image is removed or cannot be opened, Tessera uses Mosaic.
The saved choice is retained so restoring the image restores your selection.
