# Individual-card trading

Album merchants offer “I'd like to trade Tessera cards.” after the player acquires an album. This includes general-goods merchants discovered from mod-added service factions. A merchant must have a real vendor chest; Tessera does not invent a separate purse. Revyn Sadri at Sadri’s Used Wares in Windhelm and Sayma at Bits and Pieces in Solitude are the first two specialist dealers.

The Buy, Sell and Buy back tabs share the album’s controls, rarity colors and card inspection. Search includes card names, groups, affinities and the word “foil.” Tier and rarity filters apply to each tab. The Sell tab also offers Surplus only: keep two standard copies of every identity, excluding foils and uniques. The player can sell any finished card, including Tier VIII–X Legendary uniques.

Each offer starts with one copy. Tier and rarity are rolled independently, then an eligible identity is selected without repeating an identity in that restock. Pending, unique, unavailable and Legendary cards are never generated. Empty rarity/tier buckets are skipped rather than filled with a different rarity. In the current roster all buckets are populated.

Two regional groups have twice the selection weight of other groups within the rolled bucket. This is a preference, not an exclusion; every standard group remains eligible.

Stock is keyed to the merchant’s vendor chest, independent of their Tessera playing collection. Merchants sharing a chest share stock. A saved generation counter and chest identity seed the draw. Reopening, reloading or buying out a merchant cannot reroll it. Once the interval expires, the next visit generates one new selection; missed intervals do not generate extra stock.

Player sales add the exact normal or foil copy to resale stock. Ordinary resale expires at the next card restock. **Unique resale never expires.** Uniques in merchant stock are excluded from tournament trophy generation and deceased-owner recovery, preventing another copy from being awarded. A merchant’s ordinary Skyrim inventory reset does not erase this virtual stock.

## Prices and gold

Asking price = tier base × rarity multiplier × finish multiplier. Sale payout is 20% of asking price. Prices do not currently use Speech level, perks, barter enchantments, reputation or Speech XP.

| Tier | Base gold |
|---|---:|
| I | 25 |
| II | 45 |
| III | 75 |
| IV | 125 |
| V | 200 |
| VI | 325 |
| VII | 500 |
| VIII | 800 |
| IX | 1,250 |
| X | 2,000 |

Rarity multipliers: Common ×1, Rare ×3, Epic ×9, Legendary ×18. Foil multiplies the result by three.

Examples: Tier I Common costs 25 and sells for 5; Tier III Rare costs 225 and sells for 45; Tier V Epic costs 1,800 and sells for 360; Tier VII Epic costs 4,500 and sells for 900. Legendary uniques sell for 2,880 / 4,500 / 7,200 at tiers VIII / IX / X, or three times that for a foil.

Purchases move actual player gold into the existing merchant chest. Sales take gold from that same chest. The merchant must afford the whole transaction; buying stock increases the money available for subsequent sales. Skyrim controls its normal barter restock. Tessera does not replenish, inflate or duplicate that gold.

Buy back charges the exact sale payout for copies sold during the current visit. Leaving or loading a save ends that temporary price. Those copies still remain for sale at the usual asking price until they expire—or permanently, for uniques. Buying back a needed card does not automatically refill deck slots cleared by its sale.

## Selling cards

A sale asks for confirmation when it includes a unique, a foil, a card used in a saved deck, or cards needed to keep a playable hand. Confirmed sales clear deck slots whose copies are no longer owned.

Buyback is available during the current visit at the price you received. A unique card sold to a merchant remains in their stock until purchased. Ordinary resale stock expires at the next card restock.
