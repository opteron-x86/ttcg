Ranks are shown in top, right, bottom, left order. A means 10. Rank total is a comparison aid; placement and rules also determine a card’s strength.

Normal and foil copies share artwork and ranks. Unique cards have a one-copy deck limit; other identities allow two copies across finishes. Reserved unique cards can be awarded as tournament trophies. Unfinished Dragon and God candidates remain outside normal acquisition.

Finish is a library preview. Tessera uses the approved Prismatic finish at 72%. The game and this library share its renderer. The previous Tessera finish, Satin, Engraved and earlier experiments remain available for comparison. Visual inspiration: Simon Goellner’s holographic cards.
