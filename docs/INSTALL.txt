Tessera Trading Card Game

Install Main and Card Art through your mod manager, then enable Tessera TCG.esp.
Card Art: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/193442?tab=files

Requires SKSE and the Address Library for your game version. Runtimes before
1.6.1130 also need Backported Extended ESL Support.

Buy an album from a general-goods merchant for 100 gold. Open it with F7.
Challenge another player through dialogue or F8. Change shortcuts in Settings.
