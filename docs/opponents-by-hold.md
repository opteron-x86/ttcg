Winterhold also has a larger public tournament pool. Milore Ienth and Drovas Relvi are Regulars, keeping Solstheim’s early progression viable without first completing the stone and mine quests.

## Reading the roster

- **Standard access:** normal NPC interaction checks, reputation rank requirements and daily willingness. The NPC must be alive, enabled, nearby, non-hostile and available outside combat or conflicting scenes/menus.
- **Access / role:** lists additional Skyrim quest restrictions.
- **Card pool:** tier counts in the authored starting collection. Beginners hold 18 Tier I cards, Learners 22 with a couple of Tier II, and everyone else 24–32 cards weighted toward their rank. A match draws five cards from the persistent collection; wins, losses and replenishment can change its contents.
- **Gold cap:** maximum optional wager, based on the NPC’s means rather than card skill. Each opponent has a saved betting purse starting at three times their cap, topped up toward that amount every three game days. Wins can grow it; losses can drain it. Friendly games have no gold wager or card trade.
- **Discovery:** players appear only after a challenge is accepted. Conversations, refusals, album purchases do not reveal them. Normal journal views list known players; arriving in a hold does not reveal its contacts.

### Reputation and willingness

The journal shows the hold’s reputation title and a Wins total. The numbers below are reference information, not in-game progress displays. Friendly and zero-stakes matches are not recorded.

**Unknown → Unsung → Recognized → Respected → Famous → Legendary.** Experienced players require Recognized, Experts require Respected, and Masters require Famous. Only victories with cards or gold at stake award reputation. First / second / later victories against the same person give 100% / 50% / 25% of the opponent’s 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 base points. Recognized stops earning points from Beginners; Respected from Beginners and Learners; Famous from Regulars and below; Legendary from Experienced and below. Earned points remain yours.

Whiterun requires six points, three staked wins and two different opponents for Unsung; 14 points and six opponents for Recognized; 30 points, ten opponents and three Experienced-or-higher victories for Respected; 55 points, sixteen opponents and six Experienced-or-higher victories including two Experts for Famous. Legendary requires Famous and victories over all living local Masters. Smaller holds have scaled requirements. See [the full reputation rules](reference-guide.md#reputation-ranks).

After rank access, daily willingness uses base chances of 95% / 90% / 80% / 60% / 30% / 12% by opponent level, plus two percentage points per reputation point (up to 35), plus 35 when Famous or Legendary, capped at 98%. Hulda and children always accept. M’aiq accepts when his personal cooldown permits a game. Daily rolls are saved until midnight. A Master’s invitation grants willingness for 24 game hours.

## Travellers

| Opponent | Usual location | Level | Card pool | Gold cap | Access / role |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| M'aiq the Liar | The roads of Skyrim | Master | VI × 15 · VII × 16 · IX × 1 | 250 | Standard; M'aiq the Liar (IX) |
| Talsgar the Wanderer | The roads of Skyrim | Expert | III × 7 · IV × 10 · V × 7 · VI × 4 · VII × 1 | 250 | Standard; Open and One; no hold reputation or tournaments. |

M’aiq belongs to no hold and appears under **Travellers** in the Journal after accepting a challenge. Finding him is enough; no local reputation rank is required. Each tournament without Reverse has a 5% chance to draw him as a wildcard, provided he is available and not already entered elsewhere. This applies to all three circuits; he keeps his Master AI and collection even in a Regular tournament, and follows the announced tournament rules. His slot is never the one replaced when you enter. His games cannot spread or abolish regional rules.

He uses the tournament rule selector, excluding every combination containing Reverse. Two or three rules remain the most likely draw. His rules stay fixed through lobby edits and reloads, then change after a completed or forfeited game. The trade rule is One; his wager cap is 250 gold. After a game he waits seven game days before playing again. Your first staked win against him awards eight points of wider renown without altering any hold’s reputation.

His collection contains only tiers VI–VII and **M’aiq the Liar**, a neutral Khajiit Legendary unique in tier IX. The unique has sides **9 / 4 / 8 / 8** (top / right / bottom / left), uses the supplied artwork, and is drawn normally. Win it through the game’s card trade; it is not a separate first-win prize. Once lost it is never restocked. Tournament trophies that he wins may also enter his hands so that they can be won from him later. Other ordinary tiers and other players’ signature uniques remain owned but cannot enter his hands.

### Talsgar the Wanderer

Talsgar is an Expert with a 250-gold cap and a 750-gold starting purse. His collection and purse persist across road encounters. He uses Open and One, has no regional reputation gate, and does not enter tournaments. His games do not use M’aiq’s cooldown, tier restrictions or first-win renown bonus. Combat, hostility and active scenes still block play.

## Master challengers

All 27 Masters have one exclusive signature card in their authored collection. None of these signatures can appear in packs or the tournament trophy pool. Unique losses persist.

Masters are challenged through normal dialogue or the challenge hotkey. Local reputation, daily willingness and world access conditions still apply. M’aiq has his separate seven-day encounter cooldown.

## Children in every hold

Eligible actors Skyrim identifies as children can play friendly games with **no card or gold stakes**, after you own the album. This includes children added by other mods. Seventeen named children have Beginner, Regular or Expert practice collections; other children receive a Beginner collection. Their games do not record results. See the [practice roster and access restrictions](reference-guide.md#challenges-and-match-flow).

Aventus unlocks after his Innocence Lost reward handover, Fjotra after The Heart of Dibella, and Aeta after the Wind Stone is freed. Haming's Helgen actor is blocked until the introduction ends; his Froki's Shack actor remains available normally. Babette requires Dark Brotherhood membership. Helgi and Little Pelagius are excluded. All child challenges are hidden during active scenes, combat, sleep, restraint or ghost states, with the same checks applied to the challenge hotkey.

Children sometimes request Same, Plus, Reverse or Open for one game. Accept or decline before opening the lobby. Their wins do not count toward adult reputation.
