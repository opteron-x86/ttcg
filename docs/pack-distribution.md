# Albums and packs

Buy an album through a general-goods merchant's dialogue for 100 gold. The purchase includes eight starter cards and a five-card deck. The album is weightless and opens the collection; F7 opens it too.

General-goods merchants sell packs through normal barter. Each restock has a 33% chance of stocking none and equal chances of stocking one or two. Packs have a base value of 200 gold; Skyrim's barter prices apply.

Tessera recognizes general-goods service factions by the goods they buy and their merchant chests. Mod-added merchants using those records can sell albums and packs. A merchant's normal restock controls when packs appear.

Album merchants also [buy and sell individual cards](card-trading.md).
