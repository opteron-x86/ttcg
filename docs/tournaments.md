# Tournaments

Ask an innkeeper about Tessera tournaments to view available events. Regular tournaments are open to album owners with five playable cards and the entry fee. Invitationals and Masters tournaments arrive by courier as your reputation grows.

Speak to the named host at the event's inn to enter. Eight players compete through quarterfinals, semifinals and a final. Each pairing requires two wins; draws replay. You can change decks between games.

| Event | Entry | Champion's gold | Entry window |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Regular | 50 | 400 | Three game days from announcement |
| Invitational | 150 | 1,800 | Seven game days from invitation delivery |
| Masters | 500 | 5,000 | Seven game days from invitation delivery |

The event page shows its rules, closing date and additional prize. Tournament games use the event's rules and prize pool. The champion receives the announced reward, which can include cards, packs or foil upgrades.

You can enter one tournament at a time. Play each round from the host inn and finish before the closing date. Waiting, sleeping and travel advance that date. Withdrawing or missing the deadline forfeits the entry fee.

Regular events rotate among the nine holds and Solstheim. Invitational eligibility depends on local reputation or wider renown. Masters require a stronger reputation or championship record. Eligibility is checked when the event is announced.

Open the album's tournament history to review completed entries. Winning players can use their prize cards in later games.

For immediate test events, enable Development in the INI and use the tournament controls in Settings. See [Development](building.md).
