https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/49881
https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/118815

The textures in TesseraUI/skins are rendered from vector shapes in those mods'
menu files by tools/build-skin.py; tools/skin/recipes.json lists the source
file and shape for each texture. They are not covered by Tessera's GPL-3.0
licence. Complete upstream menus, SWF files and plugins are not included.

Keycap images are read from the player's own ImGui Icons installation
(Data/Interface/ImGuiIcons/Icons) and are not bundled.

Game board fields (TesseraUI/assets/boards/*/surface.webp and the rails made
from them) are built from images generated with the image generation tool of
OpenAI's Codex CLI; prompts are recorded in tools/board-art/README.md.
